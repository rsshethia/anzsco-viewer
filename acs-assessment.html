Post Australian Study — criteria & docs ($1,136)
  • Australian bachelor+ with IT/Data Science major closely related to nominated occupation plus 1yr (365 days) relevant work (anywhere, post-degree) OR completed ACS Professional Year.
  • Docs: 2× photo ID, qualification evidence, work evidence or Professional Year completion.
  • Note: ACS does not assess the 485 Australian Study Requirement — Home Affairs does.
RPL — criteria & docs ($625)
  • 6yrs relevant professional experience; most recent must be active or within 2yrs of application. Qualifications are not assessed.
  • Docs: ID + work evidence + two project reports (one ≤2yrs old, one ≤4yrs) + professional-currency evidence + mandatory vendor certs.
Qualification Only — criteria & docs ($625)
  • 485 stream: Australian diploma/associate degree, IT major closely related, occupation on MLTSSL. No experience needed. Vendor certs NOT accepted.
  • Professional-Year check: Australian bachelor+ to confirm ICT-major content for PY enrolment.
  • Docs: ID + qualification evidence only.
Assessment mechanics & rules
  • Order: ID → AQF comparability → ICT Major/Minor/Insufficient → closely-related test → experience deduction → outcome letter per ANZSCO (up to 3 codes, one price).
  • Counting: ≥20h/wk = full-time; <20h unsuitable; study-required experience excluded; overlapping episodes counted once (both listed suitable).
  • Evidence: employer refs on letterhead (or colleague stat dec + service/leaving certificate); payment evidence must match skill level; nothing can be added after submission.
  • Vendor certs: mandatory for RPL; optional for DevOps/Cyber codes; banned for 485 stream.
  • Appeals: Level 1 (internal, senior assessor, extra evidence allowed) within 60 days via portal; Level 2 (external) within 30 days via email, no new evidence; 2–4 wks each; fee refund only if wrongly assessed.
  • Service: 4–6 weeks standard (15 days advertised on new portal); free priority if visa deadline <12 weeks (2 wks notice); Christmas shutdown 21 Dec 2026–4 Jan 2027; no English test; 18+ only; completed qualifications only; no pre-assessment advice; complimentary 12-month ACS membership.
